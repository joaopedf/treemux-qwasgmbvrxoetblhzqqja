# TriageAI - AI-Powered Medical Triage for Under-Resourced Clinics

TriageAI is a voice-enabled AI assistant that empowers healthcare workers in under-resourced clinics to conduct rapid, accurate patient triage when physician access is limited. By combining real-time voice transcription with Claude's clinical reasoning, TriageAI transforms spoken patient interviews into structured medical assessments with urgency classification, differential diagnoses, and actionable care recommendations—helping ensure critical cases are identified immediately while optimizing scarce resources. Built for healthcare deserts, TriageAI bridges the gap between frontline workers and quality medical decision support, potentially saving lives by bringing expert-level triage capabilities to the clinics that need it most.
